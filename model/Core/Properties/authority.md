# authority  

## Summary

Authority specifies the rights, privileges, or benefits granted to a Role, defining what the role is authorized to access, perform, or receive.

## Description

Authority or entitlement is a string property that captures the specific rights, privileges, or benefits that are associated with a Role. This property defines what the role is entitled to do or receive within a given context (e.g., build, system, distribution, Supply Chain, or lifecycle). For example, entitlements might include access to certain resources, permissions to perform specific actions, or eligibility for particular benefits.
