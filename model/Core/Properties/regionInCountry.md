# regionInCountry

## Summary

Postal regions can be based on provinces, districts, or specific territories within a country, and they help organize mail distribution more efficiently.

## Description

A "postal region" generally refers to an area within a country that is designated for postal purposes, often used to categorize delivery routes, sorting facilities, or geographic areas. Postal regions can be based on provinces, districts, or specific territories within a country, and they help organize mail distribution more efficiently.
