# assetRiskRelevance

## Summary

Specifies the assessed relevance or significance of a risk to the target asset's operational, financial, or strategic objectives. 

## Description

Links an assessment to an `AssessmentLevelRelevance` instance that evaluates how critical a specific risk is to the asset's core functions, compliance posture, or business value. This property supports risk prioritization, resource allocation, and alignment with organizational risk appetite by quantifying the contextual importance of the threat beyond pure technical impact.

## Metadata

- name: assetRiskRelevance
- Nature: ObjectProperty
- Range: AssessmentLevelRelevance
