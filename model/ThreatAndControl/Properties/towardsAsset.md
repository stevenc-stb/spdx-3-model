# towardsAsset

## Summary

The asset that the threat may negatively impact or compromise.

## Description

Specifies the Asset that the Threat is directed toward. This property links a Threat to a resource, system, or data component, enabling context-specific risk evaluation and defining the target scope of the potential adverse event.
