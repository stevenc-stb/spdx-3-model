# riskEffects

## Summary

Specifies the assessed level of residual effects or consequences after initial controls are applied.

## Description

Links an assessment to a residual impact evaluation. This property quantifies the remaining harm or operational consequences that persist after existing controls have been applied, enabling the evaluation of residual risk and the need for additional mitigation measures.

## Metadata

- name: riskEffects
- Nature: ObjectProperty
- Range: AssessmentLevelResidual
