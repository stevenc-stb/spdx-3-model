# SecurityControl

## Summary

Mechanisms or processes that reduce the probability of a threat occurring or minimize its consequences.

## Description

SecurityControl are proactive measures, systems, tools, strategies, or protocols implemented to neutralize or reduce threats (hazards) that could negatively impact an organization, system, project such as financial losses, data breaches, or operational failures
