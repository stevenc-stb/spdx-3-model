# serializedArtifact

## Summary

Serialized artifact represents the serialized form of a CryptographicArtifact, such as a PEM or DER‑encoded key or certificate.

## Description

Serialized artifact represents the serialized form of a CryptographicArtifact. It is used to store the PEM or DER-encoded representation of a key or certificate.
