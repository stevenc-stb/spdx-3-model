# RequirementsStatusType

## Summary

Requirements go through different status types during their lifecycle.

## Description

Requirements go through different status types during their lifecycle. Below are common types categorized based on their progress and decision status.

## Metadata

- name: RequirementsStatusType

## Entries

- Draft: Initial requirement suggestion, not yet reviewed.
- ReadforReview: Being refined and not yet finalized .
- Approved: Reviewed and formally accepted for development.
- Retired: Obsolete or superseded requirement
