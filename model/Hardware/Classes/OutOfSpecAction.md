# OutOfSpecAction

## Summary

An out of specification action is defined in this class.

## Description

OutOfSpecAction refers to a situation where a product, material, environment, or process fails to meet the established standards or predefined specifications. This term is often used in industries like manufacturing, pharmaceuticals, quality control, and regulatory compliance
