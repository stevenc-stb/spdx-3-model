# requirementsStatus

## Summary

Requirements status refers to the stage a requirement is in during its lifecycle.

## Description

Requirements status refers to the stage a requirement is in during its lifecycle, from initial drafting to implementation and verification. Tracking the status helps manage changes, prioritize work, and ensure proper validation.
